---
title: Warranties
---

Part of the building lifecycle record (epic Baler#221, design §6).
Baler#198. Builds on [Positions and assets](/assets).

A warranty belongs to an **asset**, not a position. When a pump is
replaced, the new pump's warranties apply from then on, and the old pump's
stay with the old pump.

## What's stored

| Field | |
|---|---|
| `kind` | `parts`, `labor`, `parts_and_labor` (default), `extended`, `other` |
| `provider` | Who honours it |
| `starts_on` | Defaults to the asset's first install date ("from start-up") |
| `ends_on` | Or give `duration_months` from the start; an end that falls past a month's last day lands on it |
| `terms` | The terms, in your words or quoted |
| `source` | Where it came from: `{document_id, page, quote}`, a page in the [document store](/documents) when it lands |

A warranty entered by mistake is **voided** with a reason, never deleted.
Edits and voids are logged in `record_edits` (`urn:baler:warranty:<id>`).

Dates entered here are the user's. Nothing is read from a document and applied
on its own. Extracting warranty terms from O&M manuals (#202) produces
*proposals* that a person confirms.

## Alerts

`GET /ops/warranties/alerts?within_days=90` returns two lists:

- **`expiring`**: active warranties that end within the window. That leaves
  time to inspect, claim, or extend before they lapse.
- **`failures_under_warranty`**: a fault or Cx issue that opened, in the
  last year, on a position whose asset at that moment was under warranty. The
  repair should be claimed, not paid for.

How a failure is matched:

- A **Cx issue** carries its equipment URI (`cx_issues.equip_uri`), which is
  the position.
- A **fault** is on a point. Its position is the equipment whose key the
  point's key starts with. `urn:equip:fbf/hosp/chw-p1` owns
  `urn:point:fbf/hosp/chw-p1/status`, the same key scheme multi-site scoping
  uses.
- The asset is whichever was installed in that position **when the failure
  opened** (`asset-at`), and the warranty must cover that day.

The alerts are computed when asked. The **Assets** page shows them.

## API

Reads need `viewer`, changes `operator`.

| Route | What |
|---|---|
| `GET /ops/assets/{id}/warranties` | The asset's warranties, with `status` and `days_left` |
| `POST /ops/assets/{id}/warranties` | Add one (fields above) |
| `GET /ops/warranties?status=` | All, or `active`, `expired`, `not_started`, `void` |
| `GET /ops/warranties/alerts?within_days=` | Expiring and failures under warranty |
| `PATCH /ops/warranties/{id}` | Correct `kind`, `provider`, `starts_on`, `ends_on`, `terms`, `source` |
| `POST /ops/warranties/{id}/void` | `reason` |
