"Is the surgery suite ready for patients?" is the readiness report for the
suite's rooms (Baler#210, epic #221 §4). It is the same report for a lab, a
cleanroom, a data hall or an office floor. It runs over
[spaces](/spaces).

## What "ready" means

A room is **ready** only when all four criteria pass:

| Criterion | Passes when | Where the evidence comes from |
|---|---|---|
| `requirements` | every applicable requirement passes over its window | requirement packs (#205) and continuous compliance (#207) |
| `tests` | every piece of equipment serving the room passed its latest start-up, power-on or functional test | Cx 6 procedures and their test runs, for the room's terminals and everything that feeds them (`brick:feeds`) |
| `tab_report` | a TAB report is linked to the room | see below |
| `issues` | every open issue on that equipment, or on the room's own points, is resolved or accepted as a risk | the Cx 4 issues log |

Each criterion is `pass`, `fail` or `inconclusive`, and lists its evidence:
which test run, which issue, which document. **No evidence is never a
pass.** A room with no requirement evaluated, with equipment no test has
covered, or with no TAB report linked is `inconclusive`, and its verdict is
`incomplete`, not `ready`.

| Room verdict | When |
|---|---|
| `ready` | all four pass |
| `not_ready` | any criterion fails (a failed test, an open issue, a failing requirement) |
| `incomplete` | nothing failed, but something has no evidence |

A suite, zone or floor gets the worst verdict of the rooms under it, and
counts of each.

## Running it

```bash
curl $BALER/cx/spaces/$SUITE/readiness                 # JSON
curl "$BALER/cx/spaces/$SUITE/readiness?format=html"   # printable page
```

The printable page has the same Content-Security-Policy as the Cx reports
(nothing runs in it), and states its limitations at the top.

## What this slice has, and doesn't yet

- **Requirements are inconclusive until a pack applies.** The report takes
  requirement results from `readiness.REQUIREMENT_SOURCES`. Packs (#205)
  and continuous compliance (#207) register there. Until one does, every
  room says so, and the report's limitations say so too.
- **TAB report link (interim).** Until the document store (#201) links
  documents to spaces, the link is a field a person sets on the space:

  ```bash
  curl -X PATCH $BALER/cx/spaces/$OR3 \
    -d '{"attributes": {"tab_report": {"title": "TAB report, level 3, rev 2", "date": "2027-03-02"}}}'
  ```

  `readiness.DOCUMENT_SOURCES` is where the document store will answer
  instead.
- **Tests** are Cx 6 passive test runs. Active tests (Cx 9) aren't counted
  yet.
- A risk-accepted issue doesn't block readiness, and is listed as evidence.
