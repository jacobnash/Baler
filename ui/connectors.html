Sweep a subnet for Modbus devices

Probes TCP port 502 on every address in the subnet, from . Only sweep networks you're allowed to; some controllers log or alarm on unexpected connections.

Up to /22 (1,024 addresses) starts straight away. /16 to /21 asks you to confirm. Larger is refused.
